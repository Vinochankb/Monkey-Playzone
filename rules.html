Rules of Engagement

Know the Code

Break the rules, face the consequences. There are no second chances in the jungle.

General Conduct

01

No Hacking, Cheating, or Exploiting

Use of any modified client, cheat engine, external software, or exploiting game bugs to gain an unfair advantage is strictly prohibited. Hardware ban, permanent, no appeal.

⚠ Permanent Ban
02

No Racism, Hate Speech, or Harassment

This server has zero tolerance for racist language, hate speech, sexual harassment, or targeted abuse. Applies in-game, in Discord, and all associated channels.

⚠ Permanent Ban
03

No Doxxing or Real-Life Threats

Sharing personal information of other players, or making real-world threats, results in immediate report to authorities and permanent platform ban.

⚠ Permanent Ban
04

Respect Admin Decisions

Admin decisions are final during situations. If you believe a decision was unfair, use the appeal channel — do not argue in-game. Arguing publicly results in extended punishment.

△ Temp Ban
05

English Only in Main Channels

English is required in public voice and text channels so admins can moderate effectively. Other languages are permitted in private party chats.

△ Warning

PvP Rules

06

No RDM — Random Death Match

You must have a valid, established RP reason to engage another player in combat. Killing on sight without prior RP interaction or being in an active PvP zone is RDM.

△ 24h Ban + Kill Review
07

Respect PvP Zone Boundaries

PvP combat is permitted only within designated red zones. Check the map for current active zones. Zone boundaries are enforced by admin and automated systems.

△ 48h Ban
08

No Camping Spawn Points

Camping or griefing designated spawn areas, hospitals, or safe zones is prohibited. You may hold safe zone perimeters but cannot engage players inside.

△ 12h Ban
09

No Vehicle Deathmatch (VDM)

Using vehicles as weapons outside designated vehicle combat zones is prohibited. Aircraft griefing — flying over safe zones to shoot down — is VDM.

△ 24h Ban
10

Loot Rules

You may only loot a player you personally killed. Third-party looting while a fight is in progress is not permitted. Looting from safe zones is prohibited.

▸ Kick + Loot Return

Roleplay Standards

11

Value Your Life (FearRP)

If you are outnumbered or have a weapon pointed at your head, you must comply or face realistic consequences. Disregarding imminent death threats breaks immersion.

△ Warning + RP Review
12

No Metagaming

Do not use out-of-character (OOC) information in roleplay. This includes using Discord/streams to locate enemies or gather info your character would not know.

△ 48h Ban
13

No Powergaming

Roleplaying actions that would be physically impossible, or forcing other players into outcomes without giving them a chance to respond, is not permitted.

△ Warning
14

Stay In Character

Remain in character during active RP scenarios. Break character with /ooc prefix only. Do not break RP to argue about rules — that goes in tickets post-scenario.

▸ Kick (Repeated = Ban)
